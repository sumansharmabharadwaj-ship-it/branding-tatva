## The five standing behaviors (Volume I's universal checklist)

Applied to every change, every session:

1. **Explain visually before verbally.** Diagrams, decision trees, timelines, and
   interactive comparisons before paragraphs (ch.6). When a paragraph explains a
   relationship, ask whether it can become a diagram, an animation, a comparison,
   a story, or an exploration — and if yes, build that.
2. **Interactions only when they improve understanding.** Never decoration.
3. **Keep booking friction low.** Nothing cinematic may stand between a ready
   visitor and the calendar.
4. **Measure performance after changes.** The fatigue audit, the QA sweep, and
   Speed Insights are the instruments; run them after material changes.
5. **Document design decisions.** This codebase's convention: the rationale lives
   as a comment at the component it governs, written the day the decision lands.
