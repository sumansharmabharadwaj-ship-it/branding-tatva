## 7. Research summary (live sweep, Aug 2026 — primary sources only)

Patterns extracted (NN/g, Baymard, web.dev, Ragged Edge, Koto, Locomotive, How&How): progressive disclosure layered by reader intent, never hiding outcomes (NN/g); one headline outcome per case with the decision as the product (Ragged Edge); curated scarcity over volume (Koto); scope defined by example — tags plus one plain engagement sentence linking to a real project (Locomotive/How&How); comparison as decision support — few consistent attributes, differences foregrounded, sticky option names on mobile (NN/g/Baymard); price transparency as the number one information need — starting prices reduce bounce (NN/g, already satisfied by the live price book); proof attributed and placed adjacent to the claim it supports (NN/g); everything tap first and under 200ms (web.dev INP). Full sourced report retained in the session log. No interactive-diagnostic pattern was verifiable from primary sources, so ours stands on the transparency of its own scoring instead.
