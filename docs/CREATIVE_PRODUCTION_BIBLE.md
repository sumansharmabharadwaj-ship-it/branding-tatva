Branding Tatva — Creative Production Bible (Executive Edition)

A master creative direction brief for building a cinematic, conversion-focused, interactive branding website.

Creative North Star

Build a digital experience rather than a marketing website. Every page should feel like a cinematic editorial chapter. Visitors should move from curiosity to recognition, trust, conviction and conversation.

Design Pillars

Editorial composition over templates.Storytelling before selling.Motion that explains.Real proof before persuasion.Quiet luxury.Unified design language.Accessibility and performance.

Conversion Framework

Attention → Recognition → Proof → Authority → Education → Risk Removal → Invitation → Conversation.Every page should quickly answer what you do, who it's for, why trust you and what to do next.

Visual Direction

Use architecture, typography, paper, glass, wood, stone, tactile materials and restrained nature. Avoid repetitive forests and generic stock imagery.

Motion Direction

Prefer CSS Sticky, Lenis, Framer Motion, measured scroll progress and editorial transitions. Avoid scroll hijacking and unnecessary pinning.

Premium Resources

Photography: Unsplash, Pexels, Mixkit, Coverr.Textures: Poly Haven, AmbientCG.Icons: Lucide, Phosphor.AI Images: FLUX, Firefly, Leonardo.AI Video: Higgsfield, Runway, Luma, Kling.

Interactive Ideas

Decision maps, project explorers, diagnosis tools, sticky storytelling, comparison sliders, timeline reveals, contextual CTAs and dynamic previews.

Animation Rules

Use organic easing cubic-bezier(0.22,1,0.36,1). Reveal content first, effects second. Target 60 FPS with reduced-motion support.

Content Policy

Never fabricate testimonials or client results. Use clearly-labelled concept projects when demonstrating capabilities.

Claude Workflow

Audit, redesign hierarchy, add motion, optimise assets, test accessibility, run TypeScript, ESLint and production build, deploy to preview only.
