## Hidden Cost causal model

The deployed visualizer communicates a qualitative sequence. Exact percentages were removed because the supplied source set does not establish a defensible numeric relationship between marketing effort and retained brand memory.

| Website statement | Evidence scope | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Consistent long-term communication can reinforce unique and memorable brand associations. | Directional support for the sequence; individual brand outcomes require measurement. | https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0167811622000465 |
| Distinctive assets contribute to mental and physical availability and benefit from long-term development and protection. | Directional support for the stable-foundation line. | https://marketingscience.info/learn-with-us/commercial-research/distinctive-asset |

## Release rule

- Percentages and performance claims require a named dataset, unit, timeframe and source.
- Illustrative diagrams carry an explicit qualitative label.
- Research links stay adjacent to the visual claim.
