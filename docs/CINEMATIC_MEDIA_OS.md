Codified from Suman's two strict directives (Aug 2026). CURRENT truth. This governs every future asset selection, grade, transition, and interaction. It supersedes casual sourcing entirely.

## Selection law

1. **Search for emotions, never assets.** "The feeling of discovery," never "a mountain." Use film vocabulary in queries: "volumetric sunlight cedar forest cinematic," "IMAX alpine sunrise 8k," "macro dew leaf sunrise shallow depth."
2. **The 80/20 rule.** Of 100 clips: 80 rejected instantly, 15 candidates, 5 detailed review, 1 selected. Never the first good clip.
3. **The Netflix test.** Pause any frame: would Netflix title card it, Apple keynote it, NatGeo publish it, Kinfolk print it across two pages? Any no → reject.
4. **The 5 second rule.** First five seconds must create curiosity or discard.
5. **The 7 layer test.** Foreground, midground, background, atmospheric haze, directional light, motion, texture — a premium shot carries most of them.
6. **The movement pyramid.** Reject single motion footage; clouds, water, trees, light, mist, and camera should all breathe.
7. **Banned:** shaky GoPro, travel vlog, tourist footage, dull or muddy color, static frames, anything that reads as untouched stock.

## Grade (every clip, no exceptions)

Cinematic LUT direction: warm highlights, deeper shadows, subtle bloom, film grain, soft vignette, light rays where earned, slow motion where it serves, noise cleanup, dynamic crop. Palette: warm ivory, golden sunlight, forest green, moss, river blue, deep charcoal, earth brown, wet stone, morning fog, cedar, fresh leaves, crystal water. Vibrant without saturation; the site should feel oxygenated.

## Continuity and transitions

- **Color continuity:** each boundary shifts at most 10 to 15% of the palette, 20% saturation, similar luminance.
- **Invisible cut:** carry one motion across different subjects (ripple → ripple → ripple).
- **Shape match cut:** carry one silhouette (peak → triangle → roof → fold).
- **3 second breath:** between major sections, a beat of mist, wind, particles, minimal type, zero information.
- **Scroll tempo:** alternate slow, faster, slow, fast, slow — hero slow, services faster, case study slow, statistics fast, CTA slow.

## Composition law

- **Depth stack over video, always:** video → ~20% shadow → ~10% grain → gradient → blur where needed → typography → particles → cursor. Type never sits raw on footage.
- **One hero shot per section**, never ten good ones.
- **70/20/10:** 70% nature, 20% craftsmanship, 10% business.
- **Film poster test:** strip a section to one image, one sentence, one logo — it must still hold.
- **Eye path:** faces → movement → contrast → light → type → buttons; guide with moving light toward the CTA, never arrows.
- **Multi source fusion** allowed: sky, mountain, mist, birds, particles from different clips under one grade.

## The living website

Four independent motions minimum per screen (clouds, leaves, particles, cursor, type, water). Earth breathes: pollen, distant birds, rolling fog, swaying grass, moving shadows, dust in beams. Hidden discoveries rewarded, never announced. Memory test: after closing the tab, the visitor should recall the hero, one transition, one animation, one line, one feeling.
