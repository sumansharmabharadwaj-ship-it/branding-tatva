## 2. Locked project identity

Branding Tatva is a philosophical and commercial branding practice led directly by Suman Sharma.

The experience must remain:

- Rooted in Indian elemental philosophy.
- Calm, contemplative, and Himalayan in emotional scale.
- Alive through real nature, diagrams, light, texture, and movement.
- Commercially clear enough to earn consultation enquiries.
- Directly authored by Suman from diagnosis through delivery.
- Built around psychology, language, strategy, identity, websites, content, and implementation.

Nature is the operating logic, rather than a decorative skin:

- Earth decides the foundation.
- Water connects the experience.
- Fire creates distinction.
- Air carries language.
- Space compounds recognition.

## 7. Default motion contract

The page remains still in position unless the visitor activates Play Journey.

Every visible chapter must continue moving in place:

- The primary film plays muted and inline.
- A diagram, orbit, path, graph, gauge, typographic state, or light field advances.
- Deliberate interaction pauses the local sequence long enough to read.
- Local autoplay resumes after the reading hold.
- Offscreen motion and media rest.
- Poster frames and final states exist for reduced motion.

Play Journey is optional:

- It begins only after explicit activation.
- It travels chapter by chapter.
- Wheel, touch, pointer, keyboard, and form focus return control immediately.
- Every dwell must be long enough for the local chapter to explain itself.

## 8. Media budget

Mobile:

- One dominant active film per chapter.
- Additional fragments remain poster-only or absent.
- No film may sit over the main reading column.

Ordinary desktop:

- One dominant film plus one meaningful supporting film at most.
- Peripheral constellations stay away from the ladder and core copy.

Wide desktop:

- A second fragment may appear when it adds context and the composition has genuine space.

Every media asset requires:

- A purpose tied to the chapter’s question.
- A poster.
- Muted inline playback.
- Explicit `.play()` recovery.
- Offscreen pause.
- Reduced-motion treatment.
- A mobile crop or a deliberate decision to omit it.

## 9. Conversion guardrails

Every chapter answers one visitor question:

- Opening: why should I keep looking?
- Diagnosis: what is actually failing?
- Evidence: can this judgement be trusted?
- Studio: why is Suman the right author?
- Three Paths: what should be built next?
- Framework: how do the five forces work together?
- Five Elements: what does each Tatva govern in depth?
- Process: what happens after I say yes?
- Questions: what practical uncertainty remains?
- Begin: what is the safest next action?

CTA rules:

- One primary action per decision point.
- The label must state the real destination or outcome.
- Diagnosis leads into proof before asking for a conversation.
- Proof links lead to real case records.
- The final invitation may adapt to the visitor’s diagnosis or latest chosen path.
- Booking remains calm, specific, and low pressure.

## 10. Proof and fact gate

Never publish:

- Invented dates.
- Unconfirmed partner logos.
- Revenue or return claims without evidence.
- Awards or certifications not recorded in the fact bank.
- Guaranteed outcomes.
- A metric detached from its real project context.

The strongest verified public outcome remains the Dr. Haley Nutrition engagement-rate movement from 0.71% to 2.81% during the documented period.

Academic authority must always become client value:

- Clinical Psychology → perception, tension, behaviour, diagnosis.
- English Literature → voice, narrative, symbolism, rhythm, memory.
- Direct strategy leadership → usable positioning, identity, website, content, and implementation systems.

## 13. Change admission test

A new idea enters Home only when all answers are clear:

1. Which visitor question does it solve?
2. Which existing element does it replace or strengthen?
3. What does the visitor learn from it?
4. How does it move the visitor toward trust or consultation?
5. What happens on mobile?
6. What happens with reduced motion?
7. What is its active-media cost?
8. Does it preserve serenity?
9. Does it still feel unmistakably like Branding Tatva?
10. Can the result be verified in a screenshot and a production build?

Ideas without ten satisfactory answers stay in backlog.
