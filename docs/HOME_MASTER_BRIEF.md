## 3. Homepage job

The homepage is both a philosophical brand experience and the primary commercial entry point for Branding Tatva.

It must make a visitor feel:

- Calm enough to keep exploring.
- Intellectually rewarded within a few scrolls.
- Understood before being sold to.
- Certain that Suman can diagnose the real problem.
- Confident that the work combines strategy, psychology, language, design, and implementation.
- Curious enough to continue.
- Safe enough to begin a conversation.

The conversion journey is:

**attention → recognition → diagnosis → proof → authority → path selection → framework → depth → process → practical clarity → action**

Or in plain language:

**recognise the problem → see proof → trust Suman → choose what to build → understand the method → resolve doubts → book a conversation**

## 4. Brand essence

Branding Tatva is a philosophical branding practice originating in India, shaped by the elemental logic of nature and the contemplative scale of the Himalayas.

Nature is not decorative stock imagery. It is the governing logic:

- Earth = positioning, foundation, evidence, belief.
- Water = journey, experience, flow, continuity.
- Fire = expression, distinction, attention.
- Air = voice, language, communication, distribution.
- Space = recognition, memory, governance, coherence.

The visual world may use forests, roots, mist, rivers, dawn, stone, handmade paper, graphite, birds, fabric, mountain light, reflection, and living ecosystems. Generic office scenes, handshakes, staged laptops, corporate teams, and tourist-Himalaya clichés are excluded.

Branding Tatva is led directly by Suman Sharma.

Services represented across the site include:

- Brand strategy and positioning.
- Brand identity and verbal identity.
- Content strategy and content systems.
- Social media strategy and marketing.
- Website strategy, UX thinking, copy, design direction, and development.
- Marketing strategy, campaigns, and implementation.

The solo-practice structure is a trust advantage:

- The person who hears the problem also diagnoses it.
- The person who makes the strategy also writes the language and directs the work.

Academic authority must be translated into client value:

- Strategy turns both disciplines into a system the business can use.

Never present degrees as decorative credentials without showing how they improve a business decision.

## 7. Colour and material rules

The current branch is the colour source of truth.

Use:

- Forest, soil, moss, cedar, river, clay, saffron, sandstone, muted bronze, and ivory type.
- Layered gradients, translucent mineral glass, living footage, texture, and shifting natural light.
- Element colours as accents and signals.

Avoid:

- New global recolours.
- Bright white or cream page slabs.
- Flat solid fills that occupy an entire chapter without texture, footage, diagram, light, or material depth.
- Near-black everywhere.
- Seven unrelated palettes across seven sections.
- Artificial neon, oversaturated green, and generic luxury gold.

Ivory and light neutral may appear in typography, fine borders, paper details, and controlled translucent surfaces, not as blank dominant canvases.

## 8. Motion contract

### Default experience

The page does not drag the visitor downward by default.

Every visible chapter must self-play in place:

- The dominant film plays muted and inline.
- A diagram, path, gauge, graph, orbit, signal, or typographic system advances.
- Ambient light or environmental motion continues while the visitor rests.
- Offscreen motion sleeps.
- Deliberate interaction pauses the local sequence long enough to read.
- The sequence resumes automatically after the reading hold.

### Play Journey mode

Play Journey is explicit and optional.

When activated:

- The page advances chapter by chapter.
- Every chapter receives enough time to complete its explanation.
- Wheel, touch, pointer, keyboard, form focus, or control interaction pauses immediately.
- The visitor can resume from the current chapter.
- The control remains visible but must never cover core content.

### Media budget

- Only visible or near-visible films may decode.
- One dominant active film per mobile chapter.
- No more than two meaningful active films on ordinary desktop widths.
- Poster frames appear immediately.
- MP4 and WebM are preferred when available.
- Offscreen loops pause.
- Reduced-motion visitors receive stable posters and final diagram states.
- Avoid heavy WebGL, uncontrolled multi-video playback, and animation for animation’s sake.

### Motion properties

Prefer:

- Transform.
- Opacity.
- SVG path movement.
- Clip-path used sparingly.
- Slow camera push.
- Light sweep.
- Controlled blur only during short entrances.

Avoid:

- Layout-thrashing animation.
- Constant large filter animation.
- Scroll hijacking.
- Excessive GSAP pinning.
- More than the established sticky budget.
- Simultaneous competing autoplay systems.

## 9. Section contract

### Opening

Job: earn attention and explain the promise.

Must include:

- Clear headline.
- Above-fold strategy CTA.
- Work/proof path.
- Living forest film.
- Recognition experiment or equivalent teaching signal.
- Clear way to enter Diagnosis.

### Diagnosis

Job: help the visitor recognise the gap before services are presented.

Must include:

- Three recognisable business states.
- A moving diagnostic instrument.
- Strategic consequence.
- A handoff into Evidence, not an early hard sell.

### Evidence

Job: prove judgement, not merely display deliverables.

Every active file should communicate:

**signal → decision → proof**

No invented outcomes. The strongest verified outcome appears early.

### Studio

Job: prove why Suman is the right author of the work.

Must connect:

**discipline → way of thinking → client output → verified application**

### Three Paths

Job: explain what should be built next.

Diagnosis and service selection must remain distinct:

- Diagnosis = what is failing.
- Path = what to build next.

The visitor’s previous selection should carry forward.

### Tatva Framework

Job: show five strategic forces working as one system.

Must explain each force, not merely decorate with elemental imagery.

### Five Elements

Job: deliver immersive depth.

Autoplay and scroll steering must not fight. Only the active film plays.

### Process

Job: remove the black box.

Must show:

- Decision sequence.
- What each stage produces.
- What each stage prevents.
- Readiness and clarity changing over time.
- A calm, direct enquiry path.

### Questions

Job: remove practical uncertainty.

Must clarify scope, timing, implementation, distance, and fit without becoming a static accordion card.

### Final invitation

Job: make the next step feel specific, safe, and deserved.

The final invitation should remember the visitor’s earlier diagnosis or path choice.

## 10. UI and UX rules

- Every decorative element must explain, orient, prove, or reward.
- The fixed chapter ladder must not cover portraits, captions, diagrams, or conversion controls.
- Mobile fixed controls must never overlap each other or content.
- Headings, diagrams, and cards must fit inside the viewport without horizontal clipping.
- CTA labels must state the actual destination or outcome.
- Interactive systems need visible active states.
- Auto-advancing systems need manual controls.
- Hover must never be the only way to understand or operate a component.
- Mobile must receive purpose-built compositions, not compressed desktop diagrams.
- Most paragraphs should stay below roughly 70 words.
- Alternate impact, explanation, interaction, proof, and breathing space.

## 11. Copy rules

Copy should be:

- Philosophical but concrete.
- Poetic without becoming vague.
- Commercial without sounding like a sales template.
- Direct enough to understand on the first read.
- Written around decisions, recognition, memory, perception, and business movement.

Avoid:

- Empty atmospheric sentences.
- Repeating the same idea across multiple sections.
- Unverified superlatives.
- Generic agency phrases.
- Long blocks that compete with motion.
- Artificially inflated outcomes.

## 12. Accessibility and trust

Required:

- Reduced-motion support.
- Keyboard-operable interactions.
- Visible focus states.
- Muted autoplay and `playsInline`.
- Sufficient contrast.
- Semantic labels and real controls.
- No automatic page travel without an explicit Play Journey action.
- No form or booking interaction interrupted by autoplay.
- Direct-email fallback on enquiry paths.

## 13. Performance and technical rules

- Production build must pass before a preview is described as ready.
- Offscreen video must pause.
- Hidden carousel or sticky slides must not be revived by a global playback controller.
- Avoid duplicate observers fighting over the same video.
- Avoid overflow rules that break sticky positioning.
- Reserve media aspect ratios.
- Keep mobile first-load and runtime motion under review.
- Every new scene must have a poster and reduced-motion state.
- Every fixed interface must be tested at 320, 375, 390, 430, 768, 1024, 1280, 1440, and 1920 widths.

## 14. Deployment safety

- Work only on `reimagine-project-moves` unless the user explicitly changes branches.
- Create a snapshot before a major structural pass.
- Preview first.
- Production remains untouched until explicit approval.
- Never describe a commit as deployed until Vercel reports `READY` for that commit.
- Record blocked builds honestly.

## 15. Definition of done

A homepage release candidate is ready only when:

- Every chapter has one clear job.
- Every visible chapter moves without assistance.
- Local autoplay completes a meaningful cycle.
- Manual input wins immediately.
- No fixed UI covers content.
- No horizontal overflow appears on mobile.
- No scene boundary looks like an accidental spacer or empty component.
- Proof is visible early.
- Copy remains cohesive from hero to final invitation.
- All video playback controllers agree on active versus hidden media.
- Reduced motion, keyboard, and mobile states work.
- Build and static generation pass.
- The preview is reviewed before production promotion.
