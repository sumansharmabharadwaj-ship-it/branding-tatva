# The Living Ecosystem — media and narrative direction (Suman, Aug 2026)

This is the authoritative brief for all footage, grading, and section
storytelling. Every frame must answer: "Why is THIS shot here?" If the
answer is "because it's beautiful," reject it. Nature is the operating
system of the narrative, never decoration.

## Environmental psychology map (subconscious business emotion)

Forest = long-term growth, trust, legacy (deep moss, dark pine, olive, warm shadows) → strategy, about, vision, partnerships.
Sunrise = innovation, possibility, momentum (golden, soft orange, warm cream, fresh green) → hero, launches, transformation.
River = flow, efficiency, adaptability (teal, blue green, silver reflection) → UX, systems, customer journey.
Mountains = authority, leadership, market position (slate, granite, mist blue, evergreen) → positioning, competitive advantage.
Tree rings = experience, wisdom, craftsmanship (walnut, oak, mahogany, amber) → experience, case studies, history.
Roots = research, strategy, brand DNA (earth brown, clay, soil, dark moss) → discovery, workshops, foundations.
Leaf veins = systems thinking, brand architecture (fresh green, lime highlights, natural whites) → messaging frameworks, design systems.
Dew = precision, care, quality — luxury, never decoration (forms only under perfect conditions) → craft, process, execution.
Bamboo = adaptability, strength, resilience (soft green, natural beige) → startups, scaling.
Ocean = opportunity, scale, depth (deep blue, seafoam, turquoise) → market expansion, vision.
Fog = discovery, curiosity, clarity emerging (muted grey, forest green, soft white) → brand discovery, research, reveals.
Moss = quiet growth, patience, organic success → long-term growth, equity.
Rain = renewal, transformation → rebrands. Wind = influence, the invisible force seen only through its effects → branding itself.
Fire = passion, bold ideas — use sparingly (copper, orange, amber, dark charcoal) → campaign launches only.
Stone = reliability, endurance → enterprise. Flowers = expression, differentiation → creative, identity, campaigns.
Sky = freedom, infinite possibility → innovation, future vision.
Seasons = the full brand lifecycle: Spring (discovery/hero), Summer (growth/services), Autumn (results/case studies), Winter (clarity/contact).

## Cinematic DNA (every clip)

4K+ source, 1080p+ delivery, crf 20–22. Golden hour, blue hour, or soft
overcast — never harsh midday. Slow deliberate camera (gimbal/slider/
drone). Natural motion only. Lush believable greens, warm tactile earth
tones, clean blues — vibrant, never neon. Macro details reveal hidden
beauty. Minimal human presence, small within landscape. Filmic contrast,
soft highlight bloom, atmospheric depth. Footage stays BRIGHT; glass
carries text (see CLAUDE.md media standard). Goal: organic dopamine —
awe, curiosity, calm — each section a new ecosystem whose imagery
deepens the story.

## Three layers of life (rule added Aug 2, 2026 — gates every future clip)
Every chapter must contain, in one frame: PRIMARY life (the cinematic subject — canopy, stream, meadow, summit), SECONDARY life (the environment moving — wind, water, fog, cloud, light), and TERTIARY life (living organisms — birds, pollinators, dragonflies, drifting seeds, insects). Footage without the tertiary layer is scenery, not nature, and is rejected regardless of beauty. Where footage cannot carry tertiary life itself, the SkyLife component and mote layers may supply it, but real in-frame life always outranks synthetic. Biodiversity rule stands: each chapter introduces a biologically different ecosystem (rainforest canopy, moss floor, alpine summit, flowering meadow, freshwater stream, wetland, open valley). Success criterion: visitors remember cinematic moments (the forest waking up, crystal water revealing stones, birds disappearing into the horizon), never sections.

## The world standard (Aug 2, 2026 — governs all pages, supersedes section thinking)
One website, one ecosystem: Home/About/Services are chapters of a single documentary. Life layers (pollen, birds, fog, light direction) carry ACROSS pages — never reset the world at a route change. Pages are built as SCENES with emotional beats, never stacked sections. Production value bar: Planet Earth / BBC Earth / Apple keynote / A24 pacing — every frame has foreground, background, and atmospheric movement plus wildlife; nothing static, every frame rewards observation. Transitions inherit motion: one ecosystem physically becomes the next (ripple→mist→cloud→mountain). The design board is baseline (60%), never the ceiling. About = the emotional heart, slower, philosophy through nature (growth, time, patience, roots, seasons, regeneration). Services journey: the environment REACTS to interaction (blooms after answers, mist parting, cursor-following pollen). Handcrafted rule: reused components must never feel reused — unique timing/depth/light per scene. Judgment question: "Would someone remember this scene tomorrow?" Quality gate before calling any page done: does it belong beside tile.pt, slight-twist.co.nz, haoqi.design. Stop only at "people would send this to someone because it is beautiful."
