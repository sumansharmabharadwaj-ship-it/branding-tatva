# Branding Tatva Homepage Visual Audit

Audit date: 5 August 2026

Branch: `reimagine-project-moves`

Audit basis:

- Current desktop and mobile contact sheets.
- Individual desktop captures for Studio, Three Paths, Tatva Framework, Five Elements, and Process.
- Individual mobile captures for Studio, Three Paths, Tatva Framework, and Questions.
- The active master brief, milestone backlog, QA checklist, reference bank, and fact bank.

This audit evaluates representability, chapter continuity, motion hierarchy, fixed-control safety, mobile containment, conversion logic, and fidelity to the approved Branding Tatva world.

## Severity legend

- **P0** — blocks comprehension, interaction, or release confidence.
- **P1** — visibly weakens hierarchy, tone, or conversion.
- **P2** — refinement after the release blockers are resolved.

## Cross-page findings

### P0: Fixed controls cover live content

Observed:

- The full desktop Play Journey pill sits over Element selectors and the Process decision map.
- The desktop ladder detail card can cover Akash, path outcomes, portrait space, and Process metrics.
- Mobile Explore and Play Journey form a wide lower dock over cards, diagrams, and copy.

Repair:

- Keep the ladder rail visible while folding its explanatory card until hover or keyboard focus.
- Turn Play Journey into a compact cinema button that expands only on deliberate interaction.
- Keep the mobile controls compact and separated from the reading column.
- Add safe lower space only where a chapter genuinely needs it.

Acceptance:

- No fixed interface intersects a heading, paragraph, active chart, CTA, proof statement, portrait caption, or selector at any required viewport.

### P0: Negative scene overlap leaks the previous chapter

Observed:

- The top of Three Paths contains clipped Studio copy.
- The top of Tatva Framework contains the end of the Paths CTA and bridge geometry.
- Process opens with excessive Elements selector chrome.
- Several transitions resemble bars placed over the next scene rather than an edit between scenes.

Repair:

- Remove negative bridge margins.
- Keep bridges compact and in normal document flow.
- Preserve animated roots, ripples, confluence, orbit, and warmth without allowing them to overlap text.

Acceptance:

- Every chapter begins with its own intended heading or atmosphere.
- No preceding sentence is visible inside the next chapter’s frame.

### P0: Mobile Questions clips horizontally

Observed:

- The headline can extend beyond the right edge.
- Timing and Implementation labels can sit beyond the diagram frame.
- The diagram and FAQ grid can expand the implicit mobile track.

Repair:

- Enforce `min-width: 0` and one-column containment.
- Reduce the mobile heading scale.
- Pull right-side signal positions inward.
- Give signal labels a controlled maximum width and centred wrapping.

Acceptance:

- The complete headline, supporting copy, all five labels, diagram, FAQ, audit invite, and additional-question link remain visible at 320, 375, 390, and 430 pixels.

### P1: Middle chapters become uniformly muddy

Observed:

- Studio, Paths, and Framework use related earth tones, but stacked brown overlays flatten their distinction.
- Path cards and route labels lose hierarchy against the moving field.
- Framework’s five-force copy feels quieter than the ambient river fragment.

Repair:

- Preserve the approved palette while increasing separation between forest, cedar, clay, moss, river, and mineral light.
- Reduce contour-overlay opacity.
- Use clearer glass surfaces and borders for active instruments.
- Keep ambient films away from ordinary desktop and mobile widths.

Acceptance:

- Studio reads as authored thought, Paths as a decision map, and Framework as an elemental system even when viewed in grayscale or at ordinary laptop brightness.

## Chapter audit

## 1. Opening

Strengths:

- Clear strategic promise.
- Strong forest world.
- Visible strategy and Work paths.
- Recognition experiment adds a useful learning signal.

Open checks:

- Verify the recognition control does not compete with the primary CTA on 320 and 375 widths.
- Verify text contrast over the brightest forest frame.
- Confirm the hero film starts after a browser restores a background tab.

Priority: P2 after global controls and containment.

## 2. Find the Gap

Strengths:

- Three recognisable conditions.
- Diagnostic instrument has strategic meaning.
- Earlier choice carries into later path selection.

Defect:

- The old hard-sell handoff could jump directly to Contact before proof.

Repair:

- Diagnosis leads into Evidence with the label “See how decisions become proof.”

Open checks:

- Confirm the active state remains obvious during bright film moments.
- Confirm deliberate selection pauses and then resumes.

Priority: P1 conversion integrity.

## 3. Evidence

Strengths:

- Signal → decision → proof is the right commercial story.
- Verified project outcomes and system-level claims are separated honestly.
- Direct selectors make autoplay controllable.

Open checks:

- Verify the heading lands beneath the fixed header.
- Check card contrast on an ordinary laptop display.
- Confirm only the active project film decodes.
- Confirm a manually opened file pauses archive rotation.

Priority: P1 proof visibility and runtime verification.

## 4. The Studio

Strengths:

- Psychology, literature, and strategy are translated into client value.
- Direct authorship is framed positively.
- Each discipline has a verified project application.
- Desktop triptych has a strong editorial character.

Defects:

- Mobile is long enough for fixed controls to sit over the next block.
- Top metadata can be clipped by the preceding transition.
- The ladder detail card may cover portrait space on desktop.

Repair:

- Remove bridge overlap.
- Fold the ladder card.
- Reserve modest mobile clearance without stretching the chapter into dead scroll.

Open checks:

- Review portrait crop at 375, 768, 1440, and 1920 widths.
- Observe all three discipline cycles.

Priority: P1.

## 5. Three Paths

Strengths:

- Diagnosis and service choice are distinct.
- The path map communicates decisions rather than packages alone.
- The mobile route card is more useful than a compressed desktop SVG.

Defects:

- Desktop route map is small and low contrast.
- Ambient root film overlaps the right card and ladder.
- Inactive cards become muddy rather than intentionally concise.
- Mobile cards continue beneath the fixed control dock.
- Previous Studio copy is visible above the scene.

Repair:

- Give the route map a glass instrument field and more width.
- Hide the ambient fragment below wide-desktop breakpoints.
- Increase card contrast.
- Let inactive mobile cards collapse their explanatory paragraph while the active card remains complete.
- Remove scene overlap and reserve mobile clearance.

Acceptance:

- The visitor can understand all three routes without opening Services.
- The active path is immediately visible.
- All three cards remain reachable and readable at 320 pixels.

Priority: P0 composition and P1 hierarchy.

## 6. Tatva Framework

Strengths:

- Five forces explain five different decisions.
- Autoplay and manual focus are both available.
- The coherence laboratory teaches why the system matters.

Defects:

- Akash and its explanation approach the ladder safe zone.
- The ambient river fragment dominates the right edge.
- “Enter the five-element chapter” appears before the coherence demonstration, making the chapter feel prematurely complete.
- Mobile becomes long and vertically repetitive.
- The previous Paths CTA appears above the chapter in some captures.

Repair:

- Hide the ambient fragment below wide desktop.
- Reserve a right safe zone.
- Change the first handoff to “Stress test the five-force system.”
- Keep the Elements CTA at the end of the laboratory.
- Use two-column force controls on mobile and reduce the laboratory diagram height.
- Remove bridge overlap.

Acceptance:

- The sequence reads as: understand the forces → test the system → enter the five living Tatvas.
- All five names and explanations remain readable near the ladder.

Priority: P0 sequence clarity and P1 responsive density.

## 7. Five Elements

Strengths:

- This is the strongest cinematic chapter.
- Full-screen nature and Tatva copy feel aligned with the brand.
- Direct selection and autoplay are available.

Defects:

- The persistent Journey pill covers the lower selector.
- Process can inherit too much selector chrome at entry.
- Text contrast needs verification across all five films.

Repair:

- Compact the Journey control.
- Remove negative bridge overlap.
- Preserve one active film and poster-backed inactive states.

Acceptance:

- Earth, Water, Fire, Air, and Space each complete once without scroll/autoplay conflict.
- The active selector remains reachable on mobile and desktop.

Priority: P0 fixed-control clearance and P1 autoplay verification.

## 8. The Process

Strengths:

- Decision sequence, outputs, prevention, readiness, and clarity are visible.
- The system removes the black-box feeling.
- Visual density is justified by teaching value.

Defects:

- The ladder detail card can cover right-side metrics.
- The Journey pill covers the lower decision map.
- The scene can open with previous Element chrome.
- Mobile map labels need collision checks.

Repair:

- Fold the ladder detail card.
- Compact and relocate the Journey control.
- Remove bridge overlap.
- Keep the existing right-column safe zone.

Acceptance:

- Rail, focus card, map, graph, readiness bars, and stage film remain synchronized.
- No fixed interface covers the 18% clarity state or system-readiness bars.

Priority: P0 control clearance and P1 mobile verification.

## 9. Questions

Strengths:

- The clarity-field metaphor is strategically relevant.
- Practical concerns appear before the consultation CTA.
- Auto-advancing answers keep the chapter alive.

Defects:

- Mobile headline and signal labels clip.
- Lower controls cover part of the clarity field.
- The chapter is very dark at some frames, reducing hierarchy.

Repair:

- Contain the mobile layout and pull labels inward.
- Compact the fixed controls.
- Preserve the dark cinematic field while strengthening sandstone and ivory hierarchy.

Acceptance:

- Scope, Timing, Implementation, Distance, and Fit are all visible simultaneously.
- FAQ autoplay completes and pauses correctly after selection.

Priority: P0 mobile containment.

## 10. Begin

Strengths:

- Copy remembers the visitor’s earlier choice.
- Three next decisions make the consultation feel specific.
- The tide film provides a calm ending.

Open checks:

- Verify legibility across every tide-film frame.
- Confirm the most recent path overrides an older diagnosis.
- Confirm the mobile card and CTA clear the fixed controls.

Priority: P1 release verification.

## Autoplay audit matrix

Every chapter must pass:

- Starts without hover.
- Completes one meaningful cycle.
- Pauses after deliberate interaction.
- Resumes after the reading hold.
- Pauses expensive motion offscreen.
- Returns with the correct active state.
- Uses a stable poster in reduced motion.

Special checks:

- Evidence: one active project film.
- Studio: one active discipline film.
- Paths: route, active card, and result change together.
- Framework: all five forces fit the local dwell.
- Elements: scroll and autoplay never fight.
- Process: all five instruments remain synchronized.
- Questions: answer and progress line remain synchronized.

## Release-gate summary

A release candidate cannot be described as ready until:

- P0 visual defects are absent in fresh 390 × 844 and 1440 × 900 contact sheets.
- All required viewport checks pass.
- Production build, type validation, lint validation, and static generation pass.
- No fixed control covers content.
- No mobile horizontal overflow remains.
- Every local autoplay system completes one cycle.
- Vercel reports READY for the exact commit.
- Production remains untouched until approval.
