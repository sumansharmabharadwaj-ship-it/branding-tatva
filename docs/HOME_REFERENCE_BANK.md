# Branding Tatva Homepage Reference Bank

Status: active reference index for `reimagine-project-moves`

This file records what Branding Tatva is allowed to borrow from each reference and what must remain original. Reference sites are ingredients, not templates.

## Santioni Spirits

Use for:

- Sensory entry.
- Cinematic anticipation.
- Slow atmospheric reveals.
- Desire before detailed explanation.
- Chapter boundaries that feel edited rather than stacked.

Do not copy:

- Its visual identity.
- Decorative opacity that makes the offer harder to understand.
- Atmosphere without proof or action.

## Rectangles.fm

Use for:

- Playful long-scroll rhythm.
- Tactile editorial composition.
- Layered but controlled collage.
- Frequent visual rewards.
- Fun discoveries while scrolling.
- Manifesto, proof, and lead-capture beats appearing at varied rhythms.

Do not copy:

- Its retro colour system.
- Random novelty.
- Dense playfulness that overwhelms Branding Tatva’s serenity.

## Zero University

Use for:

- Compact, decisive statements.
- Clear explanations.
- One obvious action per decision point.
- Fast comprehension.
- Conversion clarity.

Do not copy:

- Generic startup minimalism.
- Product language that strips away Branding Tatva’s philosophical depth.

## Simon Holm Studio

Use for:

- Video-first restraint.
- Work remaining the focus.
- Quiet transitions.
- Minimal navigation interference.
- Editorial confidence.

Do not copy:

- Excessive emptiness.
- Portfolio logic that removes Branding Tatva’s teaching role.

## HeyParker

Use for:

- Staged explanations.
- Interfaces that teach themselves.
- Clear transformation from research to decision to growth.
- Small systems that update automatically while remaining controllable.

Do not copy:

- SaaS dashboard styling.
- Product-demo tropes.

## Horeca Social

Use for:

- Persistent commercial energy.
- Strong service outcomes.
- Repeated but calm enquiry opportunities.
- Proof integrated into the selling path.
- Clear reasons to hire.

Do not copy:

- Loud agency-template composition.
- Overstated urgency.

## Decathlon Yestalgia

Use mainly for the Work experience:

- Immersive case worlds.
- Project-specific movement.
- Evidence embedded inside the story.
- Unexpected but usable navigation.

Do not copy:

- Campaign-specific visual identity.

## User-supplied visual references

The visual image bank points toward:

- Moss and deep forest.
- Wet bark and warm timber.
- River, reflected light, mist, and moving water.
- Mountain scale and Himalayan atmosphere.
- Terracotta, bronze, olive, clay, ochre, and dark mineral greens.
- Handmade paper, graphite, botanical fragments, contour lines, roots, and archival textures.
- Cinematic warmth without a yellow cast.
- Depth, light, and motion rather than flat dark slabs.
- Ivory typography rather than white page backgrounds.

## Original Branding Tatva synthesis

The target is:

**sensory like Santioni + playful like Rectangles + clear like Zero + restrained like Simon + self-explaining like Parker + commercially awake like Horeca + rooted in Indian elemental philosophy**

Every borrowed mechanic must pass four questions:

1. Does it help the visitor understand something?
2. Does it preserve calm?
3. Does it strengthen trust or conversion?
4. Does it still feel unmistakably like Branding Tatva?

If any answer is no, the mechanic does not ship.
