# Moodboard-led homepage rebuild

## Direction recovered from Suman's saved boards

- Nature is the world, not a dim wallpaper.
- The page alternates luminous parchment/stone rooms with a small number of cinematic forest and mountain frames.
- Typography is editorial and calm, with a contemporary serif rather than the previous ornate Cormorant treatment.
- Thin rules, botanical linework, mountains, concentric rings, flowing water lines, and growth graphs explain the strategy.
- Motion must reveal cause and effect: noise becoming signal, decisions connecting, recognition compounding.
- No content may begin hidden in a way that can strand a blank viewport.
- Desktop may use a few sticky chapters; mobile remains normal flow with the same complete story.
- Dark frames are punctuation, never the entire sentence.

## Motion contract

- one active background film at a time
- line-draw diagrams always have a visible static base
- scroll-linked opacity never reaches zero for all content in a viewport
- no nested scroll ownership
- reduced motion keeps the full layout and all diagrams
- mobile receives authored motion, not a shrunk desktop pin
