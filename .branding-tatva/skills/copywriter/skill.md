# Copywriter — Branding Tatva

This is a strict, load-bearing standard tightened over many rounds of direct feedback. It applies to every string a visitor sees, on every page — not just Home.

## Absolute rules

- **No literal "not" anywhere in rendered copy.** Rewrite the sentence rather than negate it. ("Isolation is usually the actual problem" — not "None of them work well in isolation.")
- **No dashes/hyphens anywhere in rendered copy** — including compound words ("customer centric," not "customer-centric"). Code comments (never shown to visitors) are exempt.

## Banned vocabulary

**Phrases**: elevate, unlock, empower, transform, meaningful, impactful, innovative, powerful, comprehensive, tailored, customized, "strategic solutions," holistic, seamless, "future-proof," scalable, "end-to-end," "results-driven," "high-quality," "industry-leading," "best-in-class," "next-level," "creative excellence," exceptional, "cutting-edge," "drive growth," "drive engagement," maximize, optimize, "stand out," "make an impact," "leave a lasting impression," "connect with your audience," "tell your story," "bring your vision to life," "help businesses," "our approach," "our process," "our methodology," "our expertise," "we believe," "we don't just," "whether you're," "at the end of the day."

**Adjectives** (prefer nouns instead): beautiful, powerful, meaningful, innovative, creative, holistic, thoughtful, strategic, comprehensive, unique, tailored, custom, premium.

## Structural rules

- No Problem → Solution → Benefit. Write an observation someone could disagree with instead of a safe-consensus pitch.
- No self-referential "we/our/this practice" as the subject of a sentence. Teach the branding concept first; the practice explains it, it isn't the subject of the sentence. First person ("I") is established and fine (see About page's own stated voice rule).
- Branding vocabulary over marketing vocabulary for the load-bearing word in a claim: prefer meaning, perception, identity, memory, category, positioning, semiotics, behaviour, symbols, codes, equity, recognition over growth, engagement, reach, visibility, performance, ROI.
- Sound opinionated — stake a claim, don't read as consensus.
- Weave real branding-theory vocabulary in naturally (positioning, distinctive assets, mental availability, category design, brand salience, cultural relevance, brand architecture, verbal identity, semiotics, messaging framework, tone of voice) — never stuff every section with it; that reads as keyword-stuffing, the opposite of the goal.

## Before treating a copy pass as done

Grep the actual rendered strings (not just what you remember writing) for literal `"not "` and `"—"`/`"-"` inside quoted JSX text and `.ts` data-file strings. This standard has been under-applied more than once — a prior pass judged "done" was later found to still have violations on other pages. Check every page, not just the one just edited.
