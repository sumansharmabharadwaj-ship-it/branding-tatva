# Conversion Optimizer — Branding Tatva

## The established CTA family

Reuse these verbatim rather than inventing new CTA copy per page — repetition of a recognizable phrase across pages is a deliberate pattern here, not an oversight:

- **Hero-level**: "Let's build the version of your brand people remember"
- **Mid-funnel** (after proof, before a full ask): "Let's uncover the perception your market already has"
- **Closing/booking**: "Book a Brand Strategy Session"

## Where an ask belongs on a page

One CTA at the very top and one at the very bottom is not enough — desire peaks right after a visitor sees real proof (a case study, a real stat), and a soft mid-funnel CTA belongs right after that proof, before the page moves on to Process/FAQ-style content. See Home's structure: hero CTA → (proof: Selected work) → mid-funnel CTA → Process → FAQ → closing CTA.

## Booking should feel like a strategy session starting, not a form

The Contact page already treats the enquiry form and the Calendly embed as two distinct, parallel paths (fill in what you know now vs. skip straight to a booked time), not one gated flow. A `brandStages` question ("Where is your brand right now?") on the form does real qualification work before the call even happens — extend that instinct rather than defaulting to a generic name/email/message form.

## Trust before the ask

A CTA placed before any credibility has been established reads as premature. See `brand-strategist/skill.md` for the Trust-before-Authority sequencing rule — it exists specifically to protect conversion, not just narrative flow.

## What NOT to do

No fabricated urgency/scarcity ("only 2 spots left") — this practice's own commercial-honesty rule rules it out unless it is literally, verifiably true. No dark patterns. No countdown timers, no fake social-proof tickers.
